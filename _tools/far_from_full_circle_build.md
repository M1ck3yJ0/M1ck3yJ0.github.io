# Far from Full Circle: build reference

How the built page works, for chats that change a component. The code is the source of truth for sizes, colors, timings, label placement and every word on the page; this file records the intent and rules behind it. What each chart means and what is left to do live in the brief (`posts/far-from-full-circle-brief.md` in the claude.ai project); what is left to review on real devices, and what was ruled out, close this file.

## Stack

Plain HTML and SVG, D3 7.9.0 from jsDelivr, `position: sticky` for pinning, fonts self-hosted. Beats start by the transition timing rule below, by the page's own scroll check.

## Files

In `projects/far_from_full_circle/`: the page `a_circularity_story.html`; the data file `far_from_full_circle_data.js`; the fonts (`caladea-*.woff2`, `uncial-antiqua-regular.ttf`, `fira-sans-*.woff2`) and their licenses; `far_from_full_circle_shards.js`, the ring's solver for the game and explorers (usage at the top of the file; `useLess` takes one share for every material or one per material; `reshape` takes out named shards and resizes the rest to exact shares).

The data file's blocks are `build_data.py`'s (`game`, `imports` and `examples` included), merged in with the file's own blocks kept: `center_shards` (the full disc, the reveal's `recycle`, use less's `less`, both levers' `both` with its `rec` disc after recycling 70% and its final disc) and `food_heaps`. It carries `replacement`, and each country's figures at that replacement: `extra_recycled_70pct_kt`, `rate_70pct_recycled`, `rate_70pct_recycled_if_adding`, `replaced_70pct_recycled`, `gone_without_70pct_recycled`, `recycled_share_to_close`, `less_new_material_to_close`, `not_recycled_mix` and `waste_check`, plus the waterfall explorer's `highlights` and `climate_total_mt`.

## Layout

- One build, phone first: a pinned square chart with text cards scrolling over it, pinned in bursts. Cards aim for up to 7 lines, about 270 characters on a phone.
- Wide screens start at 800 px (`min-width: 800px`): the same build in two columns, chart left, cards right. The chart takes the smallest of about 55% of the width, the viewport's height less a top and bottom margin, and 560 px; cards take a 360 to 420 px column. Past 1440 px wide, where the height allows, `--u` scales the 560 px chart, 420 px cards and 48 px gap together (at most twice), and `--t` scales the type at half that rate, chart labels included through `--cf`; up to 1440 px nothing changes. On phones, `--chart` reads the width inside `main` (`cqw`), so a classic scrollbar can't push it past the edge. The explorers and the game use the same arrangement. The hero's first card is in place at load, level with the ring, so the title is above the fold.
- Nav: no bar and no menu, at every width. On a phone each section's notes link is small and quiet. The home icon sits at the page's top left and scrolls away. On a phone the icon covers the hero chart's top left (about 37 units square of the 400-unit grid): keep text out of it in the opening.
- Transition timing: each beat's transition starts as soon as the previous card is almost clear of the top edge, or, when the beat brings a new chart, as soon as that chart is 90% visible. Transitions are timed, not tied to scroll position, each tuned on a real phone to end around when the card reaches its place at a normal scroll speed (right below the chart on a phone, centered on wide screens). Start around 600 to 900 ms; on a phone the stretch from start to place is about 170 px of scrolling. On wide screens a beat starts once its card is 260 px of scrolling from its place (a chart's first card also waits for the chart to be 75% visible), and every forward beat plays 260/170 times as long. Scrolling back quickly animates to the earlier beat's state. Reduced motion speeds up each animation to its end state.
- Optional parts (the explorers and the game) are standalone figures, not pinned. The game sits inline between the waterfall and ZIN, with no skip link. The explorers sit after the floor band, before Method and Sources and notes, each a `<details>` closed on load (white boxes like the notes' summary): its summary is a short label, a one-line teaser carrying the explorer's finding (its `h2`) and an "Open it" or "Close it" toggle; opened, the explorer shows below it.
- The breaks: between the hero and the reveal, two white bands across the page, as the closer's, unpinned, with no chart and no title: the opening (`#sec-open`: Milcah's cold open, the hook and what she set out to see), then the waste paragraphs and the guess (`#sec-buried`). The ring is split in two bursts around them: the hero's burst (`data-chart="hero"`) shows a still copy of the ring at the hero's state with every label, key and source stripped (`heroRing`, copied at load and again once fonts are in), and the reveal's burst brings the live ring back, waiting at the hero's state, with no title of its own.
- The guess (the break's last paragraph, before the reveal): one question and four buttons, styled as the game's controls, with `aria-pressed`. A tap marks the answer, shows "Scroll on for the answer." and starts the reveal's first card with it (`.guess-said`, empty until then). The ring doesn't move for it; scrolling on plays the reveal, answered or not. Its options are sourced by hand in `check_numbers_by_hand.csv`.
- The closer is unpinned paragraphs: a white band the full width of the page, with the text in a centered column of at most 720 px, in the cards' type, its section title on top and its "Notes" link at the end. It has no cards, so it doesn't count as a step. Its only figures are Ireland's two small rings, still and without labels, each with a caption under it at every width: Ireland in 2022, and Ireland with 70% of its waste recycled and the cut that closes it at 24%, the country explorer's end state, drawn by the explorer's own solver. On a phone they sit side by side above Ireland's two paragraphs; on wide screens the first is left of the first paragraph and the second right of the second, each 180 px (scaled by `--t`).
- The hero has one card: the H1, "Far from Full Circle", then the subtitle, a question ("Is the EU using the right measure for its circularity target?"), in secondary ink, then the byline, and nothing else. The byline sits under the subtitle, in 15 px secondary ink: the author, the launch date (a placeholder until launch) and "Eurostat data as of" the data file's `data_as_of`.
- The credit line closes the closer, after its "Notes" link: a hairline, then "Created by Milcah M. Joseph" and links to Portfolio, GitHub and LinkedIn, centered in 15 px secondary ink. GitHub and LinkedIn open in a new tab.
- Sections: each new kind of chart starts a section with a takeaway title, above the chart on a phone and at the top of the first card on wide screens. The ring's title is the hero's; each explorer's teaser is its title; the game's tops its text column; the closer's tops its paragraphs.
- Method and notes: after the explorers, Method, then Sources and notes, each a `<details>` closed on load; its summary is the heading, a one-line teaser and a toggle, "Show it" or "Hide it" for Method, "Show them" or "Hide them" for the notes. The explorers are the only other collapsibles. Each section ends with a "Notes" link (in its last card; in the explorers and the game, under the text or chart; in the closer, after its last paragraph) to its subsection of Sources and notes: a subsection per section headed by its title, each with a back link. Every "(Method)" in the notes links to Method. Method holds one still chart, `#method-curve`, drawn at load from the data file's `cut_curve`: the EU's cut for 24% at each share of waste recycled (blue, the same at every replacement) and the part recycling replaces at half a tonne and a whole tonne (gold, the whole dashed), with dots at 70% and where each gold line meets the blue one. A link into either opens it at its target; a back link returns to the exact spot the reader left (history back), or to its section when the reader came another way, and each closes again as the reader leaves it. After the closer, before the explorers, a floor band shows the terrazzo unfaded (fading in and out over its top and bottom 22vh), with one white card in the middle, at most 560 px wide (scaled by `--t` past 1440 px): a line linking the analysis repo, then the "About the drawings" note.
- Annotations sit inside the chart's own box, never as floating tooltips. The text's only popovers are its popups.
- Popups: an underlined phrase (`button.pop`, in the text's own font) names its notes in `data-note` (ids, space-separated; table rows are gathered under their thead), and, for an image peek (`pop-img`, a small camera icon after the phrase), a `<template>` in `data-img` holding the `<!-- IMAGE: -->` placeholder, the 3 by 2 frame and the credit. At load, `popups()` gives each a native popover (`popover="auto"`) holding a copy of its notes, an "All notes" link and a Close button, so each fact keeps one home in Sources and notes. Tap or click opens it; light dismiss and Escape close it; focus moves to Close and returns to the phrase. On wide screens with a mouse, hovering the phrase also opens it (after 120 ms, focus left where it is), and it closes 250 ms after the pointer leaves both phrase and popup; clicking the phrase or inside the popup keeps it open. A bottom sheet on a phone, a 340-unit card by the phrase on wide screens, flat white with a hairline. Nothing expands inline, so scroll triggers don't move. Each answers one question in 30 to 90 words; 15 to 20 is the aim and 26 the ceiling. One chart-level popup, the waterfall's "How exact?", sits under the chart's bottom left. `check_numbers.py` reads a phrase with its paragraph and a popup's text in its note.
- Pause: one line on a card of its own (`card pause`), set larger and centered, keeping the chart's state, after the history's crisis cards (`latest`).
- Using less by choice (`#sec-less`): a second white band in the break's pattern, after the waterfall and before the game, with no chart and no title: Pontevedra, then the heat deaths, its image peek on "Pontevedra", and its notes link to `#notes-less`.
- Each card declares the chart's whole state, so scrolling back, resizing and reduced motion all work.
- One card per item in the sequence (the reveal has three, use less and both levers two; the ribbon ends on a metal ores card, state `metals`). The break holds buried in soil and the guess. An idea is never split across several small cards.
- Caveats appear on the charts as well as in the notes: the 2024 estimate, the factory gate, recycling held fixed when material is cut, the assumed replacement.
- Section ends on a phone: each burst's pin is sized (`space()`) to end 48 px below its last card in its place, so the chart and that card scroll away together, nothing covering the chart. A last card too tall for that (at 390 x 844: ZIN and IKEA's kitchens) covers the chart, which fades out (`fade()`) over 40% of its height before the pin lets go. Every section ends 48 px before the next one's title, the game included; the floor's card follows the closer with no floor above it.
- Mobile toolbars: anything sized or triggered by viewport height uses `svh` or a pixel value that changes only with the width, never a value read on every resize.

## Chart mechanics

- Ring: past 24% (the ring explorer, the game), the arc runs into a thicker translucent second lap; in the game, past 48%, a third drawn over it. Every ring labels "24% target" at ten o'clock and the rate at two o'clock, outside the ring, "[x]% rate" or "24% reached" while it reads 24.0%, tracking the arc every frame; the game sets both labels 17 units out, clear of the knob, the others 13. Keys name marks only ("Target for 2030", "Circularity rate"), never numbers. The reveal adds a striped stretch until the ring closes while shards of the replaced materials fly off, then the disc closes up; use less restores the full disc, dims the arc over 0.6 s while the shards start leaving at 0.4 s, the rate's label fading out with the arc and back as the ring closes, removes shards (the same share of every material) and shrinks the disc; both levers restores the full disc and plays the ring explorer's route for the EU, its highlight shown from the beat's start. Shards taken out fly off to the right, over the cards on wide screens (the ring explorer's and the game's over their text). A key under the ring builds by beat; its arc swatch dims with the arc. Only the game's ring has a dot at the arc's tip, as its knob; the story's ring and the ring explorer's end on the arc's rounded end. The hero's ring carries no labels (the one exception to the "24% target" rule). The reveal's ring, waiting at the hero's state, captions the disc in two lines under the ring: "The disc is the 6.4 billion tonnes the EU consumed in 2022." and "Drawn as *terrazzo*.", the word in italics (`label()` sets `*word*` in italics); no hint above it.
- Waterfall: net arrows only on highlighted steps (the history's 2008 to 2010 and 2022 to 2024, and the waterfall explorer's), which dim the rest, drawn after the step's bars.
- History line (the waterfall's first card, `line`): the published rate at the waterfall's step years only, a vertex at each slot's edge where the running total stands, dashed with a hollow end point from the first estimated step, drawn left to right. The crisis bands (`WBANDS`) shade whole step slots, the step in which each crisis cut material use by env_ac_mfa's yearly figures (2009, 2020, 2023), named flush left inside the band above the bars, in the same place in every card; COVID's name carries its step's years. The line card's title says the rate is shown every second year, when countries report their waste. On the next card the line fades behind the bars as they build; going back, the bars go first. The two end labels stay through the turn.
- ZIN: both towers start brown, then ZIN's top floors clear to daylight from the roof down to 377.
- Ribbon: its first card unfolds the ribbons from the weight column; the minerals and biomass cards fade the other materials, each with its example tagged above the chart after its material's swatch (ZIN on minerals, IKEA's kitchens on biomass). The biomass card gives the per-tonne multiples in words.

## Explorers

- Each has its own dropdown (every EU country), unsynchronized; the waterfall explorer offers a small link to show the country picked in the ring explorer. Each plays by the transition timing rule, as a chart's first beat: once opened and the chart is 90% visible (75% on wide screens), for its own duration (2.2 s for the ring explorer, 1.7 s for the waterfall explorer on a phone), stretched on wide screens and sped up under reduced motion like every beat. Closing resets it, so reopening plays it again; changing the country plays it at once.
- Ring explorer: above the ring, "[x]% less new material" over the split; a split wider than 260 units wraps after its semicolon, and the ring and keys sit 12 units lower than the opening's to fit three lines. The country's ring at its 2022 rate, then both levers at once, as one beat: recycling rises to 70% of treated waste (or holds at the country's own share if higher) while every shard that goes flies off in one scattered run, both those recycling replaces (by `not_recycled_mix`) and the cut still needed (the same share of every material); then the disc closes up until the ring closes. The `rec` disc is only the solver's step to the last one, never drawn. Countries past 24% after recycling lose no further shards and run into the second lap. Every disc starts at the same size, meaning 100% of that country's new material use. Its key is drawn at 0.82 of the pinned rings' size.
- Waterfall explorer: axis "Change in circularity rate since 2010", fitted to each country's range but never spanning less than the history's 4.8; the axis is the same length for every country. Up to two highlighted steps per country (Romania none), each labeled with the rate's two ends and the arrow that carries the story, with a net arrow; other steps dim. Labels sit in a two-row band between the axis title and the bars, reserved for every country, highlights or not. Each label sits beside its leader, right or left, in the lower row unless two labels need both; no label overlaps another or crosses a leader. Highlights are set in `build_data.py` (`WATERFALL_HIGHLIGHTS`) and written to the data file's `highlights`, so Eurostat's update means a rebuild. The "2022 to 2024: Eurostat's estimate" caveat sits above the source line, shown only while the last step is estimated. Cards are hand-written per country in the page's `XW_TEXT`.
- The ring explorer's card, for a country past 24% after recycling that stays short of it with all its waste recycled but its soil and rock (the data file's `rate_without_soil_rock`), says so, with soil and rock's share of its treated waste.

## The ring game

- First beat: the card holds three short paragraphs of instructions (the task, the knob, the taps) and a Start button, with two curly arrows on the chart, to the knob and to the keys. Start is filled in `--control-go`, white bold text, centered under the instructions, as is "Start again"; the other controls stay pale. Start, or the reader's first move, replaces them with the numbers, keeping the chart where it is on screen; "Start again" doesn't bring them back.
- Recycling knob at the arc's tip, in the arc's colors, dragged around the ring (arrow keys 1% of waste, Page Up and Down 10%). It sets the share of treated waste recycled, from 2022's 41% to 100% in whole percents, to whichever share puts the tip nearest the pointer, and follows the drag into the second lap (up to one full lap). The disc re-solves on release; the arc and numbers follow live.
- Taps: each removes a quarter of that material's own 2022 use (`GM_STEP`, 25%), as whole shards, so four taps empty it. At 2022's recycling the ring closes in 6 to 13 taps whatever the route (minerals first the fewest, the other three first the most); at 70%, in 3 to 9. The tap that would pass 24% stops exactly at 24%. Recycling replaces first and taps cut only what it hasn't replaced, so a partial cut gives way as recycling rises; a material tapped to nothing stays gone. Keys show "−xx%" and gray out at −100%. One tap removes about 11% of the climate counter on biomass, 7% on fossil fuels, 4% on metal ores and 3% on minerals. "Start again" resets.
- On wide screens the game's title sits level with the chart's title above the ring.
- Numbers, no scales: under the keys on a phone, in the card column on wide screens. Above the ring, one line in every state: "How much less new material?" until anything is taken away, then "[x]% less new material". The split (replaced by recycling, not replaced, or "Recycling held at its 2022 level") is small print under weight removed. The multiple of 2022's recycling shows only here, two decimals below 1.1x, never "recycling rate", with the EEA's 70% for 2030 and 90% for 2050 under it.
- Ending: when the ends meet and the disc settles, the arc swells and a thin gold ripple spreads and fades (not under reduced motion), again each time the ring closes after dropping below 24%. It shows full width under the chart and numbers on wide screens (17 px type) and after the numbers on a phone (16 px). Its first paragraph leads with the weight taken away against the climate cost removed ("but only" when the climate share is smaller), then, when recycling was raised, the share recycled and its multiple of 2022, with each recycled tonne replacing half a new one. The second opens with the comparison, heaviest first at the same recycling and weight, only when the reader's route differs from it, and ends the same for every route (`GM_HANDOFF`): the rate counts only weight. Imports and the split stay in the numbers.
- Each tap or release re-solves with `reshape` in a Web Worker (on the page where no worker starts); leaving shards fly off as in use less, then the new layout blends in. Solves take under 0.1 s on a laptop. `FFC.game` plays it by script.

## Look

Colors live only in the page's CSS, as variables named by role.

- Page: light, white base, a terrazzo print that scrolls with the page (one seamless 1600-unit tile, drawn 900 px on a phone and 1280 px on wide screens, anchored to the page's top left) behind a mask fixed to the screen. On a phone it fades to almost nothing behind the pinned chart and is clear below it; on wide screens it is clear in the side margins and fades to almost nothing across the chart and cards. The chips come in two tones from the same tile: relative luminance 0.83 to 0.87 behind the story on a phone, and 0.71 to 0.77 behind the story on wide screens and in the floor band at every width. Only the small flecks are darker.
- Roles: blues for material used, one per material (minerals EU blue, metal ores sky, fossil fuels azure, biomass cobalt); the waterfall's "used less" bars a deeper blue of their own (`--wf-used`), apart from every material's; lighter gold for what comes back; deep gold for the 24% target; a darker deep gold for the second and third laps. Building and object tones (ZIN, the balance, the food) are not series colors. Controls have their own variables; the knob keeps the arc's colors.
- Text: ink and secondary, never in a series color, never on the shards. White on EU blue and cobalt, ink on gold.
- Highlights in the closer: `mark`, a band of `--highlight` (the lighter gold at 40% on white) under the lower part of the line, ink text (13.3:1). Not a series color.
- Flat fills, no outlines or shadows, square white cards with no outline. Rounded ends only on the ring's arc, pointed ends only on the waterfall's bars; hatching only for estimates and recycling added in a scenario, and on a line, dashes only for estimates. Page text never names the hatching.
- The ring's and the heaps' headline figures are whole percents; a split under one keeps one decimal so its parts add up. The explorers and the game keep one decimal.
- Labels that pair up line up, such as a name level with the midpoint of its two shares.
- Every chart's title, with any line under it, and its source lines are centered across the chart.
- Type: Cambria where the reader has it, else Caladea 1.002 (Google's crosextrafonts-20130214 release, Apache 2.0), which matches Cambria's character widths; never a later Caladea, whose widths differ. The H1 is Uncial Antiqua, its "C" in the target circle's gold (`--target`), served as the unmodified file from Google Fonts, never subset or converted. All chart text is Fira Sans (regular, italic, bold; Latin subset); chart titles (class `lab-title`: each chart's headline or title line, the waterfall's axis title and the ribbon's column heads) are Fira Sans bold. Everything else, the subtitle, section titles (bold), explorer teasers, notes, game keys and counters included, is in the body font. Card text (cards, the explorers' and game's text, the closer) is 18 px on a phone and 20 px on wide screens. Chart text scales with the chart, except on wide screens, where it is capped at 1.075 px per viewBox unit (`--cf`, set once at load), so labels stay smaller than the card text.

## Checking

- `check_page.py` opens the page as a file (font blocked, the ring explorer and the game solving on the page, so mid-flight states are expected). `check_steps.py` serves the page and checks it card by card, each scrolled to its place, with reduced motion; its screenshot can catch both levers' beat mid-way. Usage at the top of each file.
- `check_numbers.py` is the final number check, one command against the analysis repo's tables. It renders the page and reads every figure a reader can see, collapsed notes, chart labels in every card's state, both explorers for every country and two game routes included, and matches each to one named row by its section, unit and nearby words as well as its value. It prints only problems: figures with no row and no entry, ambiguous matches, rounding that disagrees with the nearest row, a rounded match sharing no word with its paragraph, and by-hand entries no longer used. `check_numbers_by_hand.csv` holds what the tables can't settle, keyed on section and figure (with an optional word in the clause): a row chosen among several, or a figure checked by hand, with its F number and reason. Each section's rows are set in its `SCOPES`, by section id: a new section needs a line there. Keep section ids, cards' `data-state`, the explorers' selects and `FFC.show` and `FFC.game` stable, since the check finds the page's parts by them, not by its words.
- Only `check_numbers.py` steps through the explorers' countries and plays the game, and only for their words and numbers: render each separately when its charts change (the game through `FFC.game`, the ring's states through `FFC.draw.ring`). Check transitions separately.
- If the weight shares, the replacement, or the EU's 2022 circular use or consumption change, rerun `make_shards.py` (`--check` re-measures) and check all five discs by eye (full, the reveal's, use less's and both levers' last; its `rec` disc isn't drawn). After any change to `make_heaps.py`, check its printout (42.2% exactly) and `--preview`.

## Review on real devices

Guideline misses and checks left from the build, for step 17. Tick each off here when done.

- Card length: every card carries the full second draft, over 270 characters (the reveal's first card about 900, its third about 690, both levers' card and the ring explorer's text about 590 for Germany are the longest; ZIN's first card and its paradox card about 115 words; the biomass card's per-tonne sentences and the history's first two cards are Claude's first draft, the split card about 85 words; the 2008 to 2010 card about 85 words with its cost sentence). Target is 50 words or fewer, after Milcah's rewrite.
- Type: card heights, line breaks and transition timings were tuned on Newsreader; recheck them on a real phone with the new type. Only Caladea has been rendered so far: check real Cambria on a computer with Office, and the Uncial Antiqua title on a large monitor (it breaks onto two lines, "Far from / Full Circle", at every width). Some chart titles were regular weight before and are now Fira Sans bold: check none crowds a label, on a phone especially.

Past 1440 px the type grows at half the layout's rate (cards about 23 px at 1920 x 1080, 28 px at 2560 x 1440): check on a large monitor whether it should grow faster.
- Waterfall explorer: the axis is the same length for every country, so Romania, which has no highlights, shows an empty label band above its bars.
- Transitions: tune each beat on a real phone; check fast flings and scrolling back through each burst. The explorers run 3.4 s and 2.6 s on wide screens; the game's ripple about 1.3 s. The history line draws in 0.9 s and its turn into the waterfall takes about 0.9 s on a phone. Time the game's solves and try its knob on a real phone. Retime after the restructure.
- Game: its title is an invitation, not a takeaway. Keep or trim.
- November 24 changes, on a real phone: ZIN's longer title, the 2008 to 2010 card's length, and Ireland's two rings (the closed one is much smaller, by design: check it doesn't read as a mistake). The break's band, tested in Chromium only: the gap before it, the guess's buttons (four in a row on wide screens), and the ring coming back for the reveal.

Check its scrolling on a real phone. Keep flecks very faint behind plotted data. Check the darker chips' margins on a large monitor, where they are widest, and that the floor band reads as a floor on a real phone.
- Notes: tested in Chromium only. On a real iPhone, try a section's notes link, its back link and the browser's back and forward, and a "(Method)" link inside the notes and back, and check the reader lands where they left with each section closed again.
- Explorers: each plays when opened and resets when closed, tested in Chromium only, its start and length checked by script, not by eye. On a real phone, open each, check the play starts in view, and try each explorer's notes link and back link from inside it opened (the waterfall explorer's checked by script). On wide screens the closed summaries are 640 px wide, narrower than the opened explorer below them: check it reads as one block.

The pause reads as a pause over the pinned waterfall. Fill each image placeholder, and check the frames once real images are in.
- Section ends (November 26): on a real phone, check each chart and last card scroll away together, and that the fade on ZIN and IKEA's kitchens reads as deliberate.
- The new band (`#sec-less`) after the waterfall: check the gap and the move into the game on a real phone.

## Ruled out

- A nav bar or menu, at any width.
- Keeping the browser's toolbar hidden while scrolling: no page can.
- Any movement or reaction in the terrazzo background: a shine on load (built and reverted) and a darken-on-hover.
- Waste drawn on the ring's disc, as part of the year's new material.
- A per-tonne bar chart beside the ribbon.
- Barlow Condensed, the eyebrow, and Forum for titles; oldstyle figures, which neither Cambria nor Caladea has.
